Weather

wind, temperature & rain-adjusted efficiency

Each ride is matched to that day's wind speed & direction, temperature and rainfall (via Open-Meteo's historical archive). Wind is checked against this route's compass bearing to work out whether you were riding into a headwind or getting pushed along by a tailwind. Efficiency figures in the table below are adjusted for all three: a headwind or rain boosts the credited efficiency (you worked harder for that speed than the raw number shows), a tailwind trims it, and temperature is scored either side of a comfortable ~15°C — both cold and hot days get a small boost, since both make the same speed cost more.
